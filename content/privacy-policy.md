## English

**In short.** JustInVideo works on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and the app does not ask for
internet access at all. Your videos, music, voice recordings and projects stay on your phone.

**What the app uses, and why**

- **Videos, music and subtitle files that you choose.** You pick them with Android's own file picker. The app copies them
  into its private storage on your phone so that a project keeps working after the original is moved or deleted. They
  are removed when you delete the project or uninstall the app.
- **The microphone,** only while you press Record a voiceover. The recording is saved in the project's private folder on
  your phone and is never uploaded. The app asks for your permission first, and every other feature works without it.
- **Your phone's video gallery,** to save the videos you export (the Movies folder, under JustInVideo). You can delete
  them at any time like any other video.
- **Sharing,** only when you press Share. The video is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet, and that
  app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

**What the app does not do.** It does not collect, send, sell or share personal data. It has no ads, no tracking, no
analytics and no crash-reporting service, and it asks Android for no internet permission, so it cannot send anything
over a network.

**Backups.** The app turns off Android's backup of its data, so your projects are not copied to Google's cloud or to a
new phone by the app. Export a video if you want to keep the result. Uninstalling the app deletes its projects.

**Purchase.** The app is bought through Google Play, which handles the payment under Google's own terms and privacy
policy. The developer does not receive your payment details.

**Children.** The app is not made for children under 13.

**Deleting your data.** The developer holds none of your data. To erase everything on your phone, delete the projects
in the app, clear its storage in Android's settings, or uninstall it. Videos you exported stay in your gallery until you
delete them there.

**The web version.** JustInVideo can also be used in a web browser, at video.medicom.vip. It is a separate way to use the
app, and this part of the policy applies to it.

- **Your files stay in your browser.** Videos, music, subtitle files, voice recordings and projects that you add are
  copied into your browser's own private storage on your computer (the browser's database and its private file system).
  They are not uploaded, and the developer cannot see them. The site has no account, no ads, no analytics, no cookies
  and no scripts from other sites, and its security policy lets the page connect only to itself.
- **What it remembers.** The site keeps the language you picked, the microphone you chose for voiceovers (as a code that
  your browser gives it), and a note that it has asked your browser to keep its storage, in the browser's local storage,
  and a copy of its own files in the browser's cache, so that it can open without a connection.
- **The microphone,** only while you press Record a voiceover or Test the microphone. Your browser asks for your permission
  first. You can choose which microphone is used; the list of your microphones is read in your browser, to show it to you,
  and goes nowhere. The test and the level bar while you record only measure how loud the sound is, on your device, and
  keep nothing. The recording stays in your browser and is never uploaded, and every other feature works without it.
- **Exporting.** The video is made inside your browser and offered as a download to your device. If you press Share, where your
  browser offers it, the video is handed to the app you choose in your device's share sheet, and that app's own privacy policy
  applies from then on. If an export fails you can copy a few lines about the problem, with nothing of your files in them, to
  paste into a message to us; nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.
- **The host.** The site is served by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub receives your browser's request for the
  page, including your IP address, and handles it under GitHub's own privacy statement. The developer does not receive
  this and adds no tracking of its own.
- **Deleting your data.** Delete a project in the site to remove it and its files from your browser. Clearing the site's
  data in your browser's settings removes everything. Your browser may also clear a site's storage when your computer
  runs short of space, unless you have allowed the site to keep it. Videos you exported stay on your device until you
  delete them.

**Changes.** If a later version adds an optional feature that sends anything off your phone, such as a cloud service,
this policy will be updated before that version is released, the feature will be off until you turn it on, and the app
will tell you what would be sent.

**Questions.** Write to justinvideo@medicom.vip.
